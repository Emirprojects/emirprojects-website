Briefing to installation

One process. Clear accountability.

  1. 01

    Brief & specification

    Introductory meeting, design, BOQ and finish review. Project definition, roles, approvals and site constraints.

  2. 02

    Sourcing & value options

    Manufacturer matching, quotations and value alternatives that protect the approved intent.

  3. 03

    Samples & approvals

    Coordinated submittals, samples and finish schedules with documented approvals.

  4. 04

    Procurement & production

    Order placement, production tracking, milestone follow-up and pre-dispatch inspection.

  5. 05

    Logistics & delivery

    Packing, labelling, freight, customs and sequenced site delivery.

  6. 06

    Installation & close-out

    Installation coordination, handover, snagging and close-out support.

ApproveControl the intent

Approved specifications, samples and finish schedules. Supplier due diligence and document control.

InspectMonitor before dispatch

Production monitoring, milestone follow-up, pre-dispatch finish and quantity inspection, packing and labelling checks.

CloseTrack to handover

Delivery records, snagging and corrective follow-up. Every product verified against specification before it reaches site.
